# Mode Specifications

Full Mode vs Express Mode for write-manuscript skill.

---

## Mode Comparison

| Aspect | Full Mode (Academic) | Express Mode (Short-form) |
|--------|---------------------|---------------------------|
| **Time** | 45 minutes | 15-20 minutes |
| **Phases** | All 4 phases with deep analysis | Streamlined phases |
| **Depth** | Deep provocation and challenge | Lighter challenge, faster iteration |
| **Use Cases** | Thesis, journal papers, original research, grant proposals | Blog posts, news releases, policy briefs, executive summaries |
| **Quality Gates** | All gates fully enforced | Same gates, lighter execution |

---

## Full Mode (Academic)

### Characteristics
- **Comprehensive structure analysis**
- **Deep provocation throughout**
- **Extensive tension identification**
- **Thorough quality checking**

### Phase Specifications

**Phase 1: Ingest & Understanding**
- Complete read of all research notes
- Identify: Knowns, Gaps, Key Claims
- Deep tension analysis across sources
- Comprehensive contribution articulation

**Phase 2: Argument Structuring**
- Full consensus mapping
- Detailed tension identification and analysis
- Complete outline scaffolding with section-level claims

**Phase 3: Interactive Drafting**
- Continuous challenge and provocation
- Deep assumption surfacing
- Multiple alternative framings presented
- Thorough adjudication required

**Phase 4: Quality Gates**
- Full citation verification
- Complete consistency checks
- All checklist items verified

**Phase 5: Validation Handoff**
- Comprehensive validation suite
- All validation skills invoked

### Recommended For
- Thesis chapters
- Journal article submissions
- Original research papers
- Grant proposals
- Technical reports requiring peer review

---

## Express Mode (Short-form)

### Characteristics
- **Quick context scanning**
- **Lighter provocation**
- **Streamlined workflow**
- **Same quality standards, faster execution**

### Phase Specifications

**Phase 1: Ingest & Understanding (Simplified)**
- Quick scan for main points
- Identify key knowns and gaps (skip deep tension analysis)
- Simple contribution test: "What's the one thing readers take away?"

**Phase 2: Argument Structuring (Streamlined)**
- Quick consensus mapping
- Outline without deep tension analysis
- Focus on main argument flow

**Phase 3: Interactive Drafting (Lighter)**
- 1-2 key challenges per section (vs continuous)
- Focus on:
  1. Key claim accuracy
  2. Evidence support
- Skip deep assumption surfacing

**Phase 4: Quality Gates (Same)**
- Full citation verification (maintained)
- Consistency checks (maintained)
- Quality standards not compromised

**Phase 5: Validation Handoff (Streamlined)**
- Quick validation option available
- Focus on critical checks

### Recommended For
- Blog posts
- News releases
- Policy briefs
- Executive summaries
- Internal reports
- White papers (non-academic)

---

## Mode Selection Criteria

### Choose Full Mode If:
- [ ] Output will be peer-reviewed
- [ ] Original research contribution
- [ ] Complex multi-source synthesis required
- [ ] Tensions between sources need adjudication
- [ ] High stakes publication (thesis, journal)

### Choose Express Mode If:
- [ ] Short-form content (< 3000 words)
- [ ] Quick turnaround needed (< 1 week)
- [ ] Internal audience (not peer review)
- [ ] Straightforward synthesis (limited tensions)
- [ ] Communication over research (news, briefs)

## Time Savings Breakdown

### Full Mode: 45 minutes
- Phase 1: 10 min (deep analysis)
- Phase 2: 10 min (detailed outline)
- Phase 3: 20 min (continuous provocation)
- Phase 4: 5 min (quality gates)

### Express Mode: 15-20 minutes
- Phase 1: 3 min (quick scan) - **70% faster**
- Phase 2: 4 min (streamlined outline) - **60% faster**
- Phase 3: 6-9 min (lighter provocation) - **55% faster**
- Phase 4: 2 min (same gates, focused) - **60% faster**

**Overall Time Savings: 56%**

---

## Quality Guarantee

### Express Mode Does NOT Compromise:
- ✅ Citation verification (all claims must trace to sources)
- ✅ Consistency checks (gap-contribution alignment)
- ✅ Anti-pattern enforcement (no hallucinations, no copy-paste)
- ✅ Validation availability (can still run full validation)

### Express Mode Streamlines:
- ⚡ Tension analysis (identifies but doesn't deeply explore)
- ⚡ Provocation frequency (key challenges only)
- ⚡ Alternative framing (fewer options presented)
- ⚡ Assumption surfacing (focus on critical assumptions)

---

## Mode Switching

### Can I Switch Modes Mid-Process?

**Yes, with caveats:**

**Full → Express:**
- ✅ Safe after Phase 1 (can streamline remaining phases)
- ⚠️ Cannot "un-analyze" tensions already surfaced

**Express → Full:**
- ✅ Safe at any point (add deeper analysis)
- ⚠️ May need to revisit earlier phases for deep tension analysis

### Recommendation
Choose mode upfront based on output type and available time. Mode switching is possible but may create inconsistencies in analysis depth.
