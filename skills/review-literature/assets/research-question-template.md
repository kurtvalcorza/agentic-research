## Guidelines for Writing Research Questions

### Good Research Questions Are:

1. **Specific:** Narrow enough to be answerable
   - ❌ Bad: "How does technology affect education?"
   - ✅ Good: "How do AI-powered tutoring systems impact student learning outcomes in K-12 mathematics education?"

2. **Answerable:** Can be addressed through literature synthesis
   - ❌ Bad: "What will education look like in 2050?"
   - ✅ Good: "What evidence exists for the effectiveness of AI tutoring in improving math test scores?"

3. **Scoped:** Defines boundaries (population, context, outcomes)
   - ❌ Bad: "Do AI tutors work?"
   - ✅ Good: "Do AI tutors improve learning outcomes for K-12 students in STEM subjects?"

4. **Aligned with goals:** Matches your purpose (project proposal, academic paper, etc.)
   - For Example Research Institute proposals: Focus on practical applications
   - For academic papers: Focus on theoretical contributions
   - For technical reports: Focus on evidence synthesis

### Question Types

#### Descriptive (What exists?)
"What implementation factors are associated with effective AI tutoring systems in K-12 education?"

#### Comparative (What's the difference?)
"How do adaptive AI tutors compare to fixed-sequence tutors in improving math learning outcomes?"

#### Relational (What's the relationship?)
"What is the relationship between personalization features in AI tutors and student engagement?"

#### Causal (What's the effect?)
"What is the impact of AI-powered tutoring on student learning outcomes in K-12 mathematics?"

### Common Pitfalls

❌ **Too broad:**
"How does AI affect education?"
→ Narrow to specific AI application, context, outcome

❌ **Multiple questions in one:**
"How do AI tutors impact learning outcomes and what are the implementation challenges and cost-effectiveness?"
→ Choose one primary question (others can be sub-questions)

❌ **Normative (value judgment):**
"Should schools use AI tutors?"
→ Rephrase as "What evidence exists for the effectiveness of AI tutors in K-12 settings?"

❌ **Unanswerable with literature:**
"What do students think about AI tutors they've never used?"
→ Rephrase as "What do students report about their experiences with AI tutors?" (requires existing studies)

### Refinement Process

**Draft 1 (Too broad):**
"How does AI help students?"

**Draft 2 (More specific):**
"How do AI tutoring systems improve learning outcomes?"

**Draft 3 (Scoped):**
"How do AI tutoring systems improve mathematics learning outcomes in K-12 students?"

**Final (Fully specified):**
"How do AI-powered tutoring systems impact student learning outcomes in K-12 mathematics education?"

## Example Research Questions by Use Case

### Example Research Institute Project Proposal (Project Atlas)
"How do AI-powered tutoring systems improve learning outcomes in K-12 mathematics, and what implementation factors predict success?"

### Academic Literature Review
"What mechanisms explain the relationship between AI tutor personalization features and student learning gains?"

### Technical Report
"What evidence exists for the long-term effectiveness of AI tutoring systems in improving K-12 STEM learning outcomes?"

### Grant Proposal
"What design features of AI tutoring systems are associated with equitable learning outcomes across diverse student populations?"
