## Example (Delete Before Using)

**Research Question:**
How do AI-powered tutoring systems impact student learning outcomes in K-12 mathematics education?

**Inclusion:**
- Published 2018-2025 (recent AI advances)
- Empirical studies (quantitative or mixed methods)
- K-12 mathematics context
- Measures learning outcomes (test scores, retention, transfer)
- Peer-reviewed

**Exclusion:**
- Opinion pieces
- Higher education
- Non-mathematics subjects
- Theoretical papers without empirical validation

**Relevance Test:**
1. Addresses AI tutoring systems (not general EdTech)
2. Measures learning outcomes (not just engagement)
3. K-12 context (grades K-12, not college)

**Expected corpus:** 20-40 papers from 50 candidates
