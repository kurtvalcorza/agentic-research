# Frame Contributions

Strategic contribution framing for research with "Tools for Thought" provocation mode. Articulates what your work contributes, what it implies, limitations, and future research directions.

## Philosophy

Research contributions aren't just about what you found—they're about what you've proven, what becomes possible, and what remains unsolved.

## Provocation Modes

**1. Full Provocation** (default)
- Deep interrogation of implications
- Questions assumptions
- Challenges framing
- Pushes for broader significance

**2. Targeted Provocation**
- Focuses on specific contribution types
- Less aggressive questioning
- Balances challenge and support

**3. Minimal Provocation**
- Structured template only
- Light guidance
- User-led framing

## Audience Focus Options

- **Balanced**: Mixed academic/practitioner audience
- **Practitioners**: Emphasizes applicability and actionability
- **Policymakers**: Highlights decision-making implications
- **Researchers**: Emphasizes theoretical advancement and gaps

## Contribution Framework

**What You Contributed**
- Empirical findings
- Methodological innovations
- Theoretical extensions
- Practical tools/frameworks

**What It Implies**
- Theoretical implications
- Practical applications
- Policy recommendations
- Paradigm shifts

**Limitations**
- Scope boundaries
- Methodological constraints
- Generalizability limits
- Alternative explanations

**Future Research**
- Unanswered questions
- Logical extensions
- New methodologies needed
- Cross-domain applications

## How It Works

1. Reads your research synthesis or draft
2. Activates selected provocation mode
3. Interrogates contribution significance
4. Challenges framing (if full/targeted mode)
5. Produces structured contribution section

## When to Use

- After completing synthesis but before final draft
- When stuck articulating significance
- Before conference submissions
- When reviewers ask "so what?"
- During dissertation/thesis writing

## Outputs

**Contribution Section** with:
- Clear statement of contributions
- Implications across domains
- Honest limitation acknowledgment
- Specific future research directions
